Delete your Glasses Finder account

Glasses Finder: Virtual Try On — published by blayza

How to ask for deletion

  1. Send an email to saadfellak0000@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Glasses Finder account".
  2. Send it from the Google account (email address) you use to sign in to the app, so we know the account is yours.
  3. We confirm by email and delete your data within 30 days.

You can also send the request from the app: tap your profile picture, open "Help and support" and write "Delete my account".

What we delete

  • Your account (name, email address, profile picture and user ID)
  • Your favourite frames
  • Your reviews of stores
  • Your support messages
  • If you own a store: the store page, its address, phone number, map location, verification photo, front photo, offers and ratings

What we keep

  • Records of purchases made through Google Play (product and purchase token) may be kept for up to 12 months for accounting and fraud prevention, then deleted.
  • Reports other users made about a store may be kept for up to 90 days for safety reasons, then deleted.

Face photos are never uploaded, so there is nothing to delete on our side; they stay only on your phone.
