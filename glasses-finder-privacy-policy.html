Privacy Policy — Glasses Finder

Last updated: 8 November 2026 (crash reports added)

This policy explains what Glasses Finder (“the app”, package com.blayza.glassesfinder) does with your information. The app is published by blayza. For any question, or to ask us to delete your data, contact saadfellak0000@gmail.com.

The short version

Your face photos and selfies never leave your phone: the face analysis and the glasses try-on run entirely on your device. You sign in with your Google account so the app can save your favourites, your support messages and, if you own a glasses store, your store page. Signing in is optional: you can try glasses without an account. Ads are shown by the advertising services listed below.

Your face photos

  • Photos you take or pick to try on glasses are processed on your device only.
  • They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we keep no copy.
  • The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo.
  • Results are saved to your gallery only when you tap Save.

Your account

If you sign in, the app uses Google Sign-In through Google Firebase. We receive your name, email address, profile picture and a user ID from Google. They are stored in our Firebase database (Google Cloud) and used to:

  • keep your favourite frames;
  • show your name on reviews you write about stores;
  • answer the support tickets you send from the app (with the app version and phone model, to help fix problems).

Glasses stores

If you register a store, we also store:

  • the store name, city, address and phone number you enter, and its map location;
  • a verification photo of the store front, taken with your location and distance to the store, so we can check the store is real. After approval this photo is shown on your public store page;
  • your store offers and the promotions you buy.

The photos of glasses you add to your store collection stay on your phone.

Store pages (name, address, phone, location, front photo, rating and reviews) are public to app users once approved.

Location

Your location is used to show glasses stores near you and, for store owners, to verify the store address. Customer location is not stored on our servers.

Purchases

Store Pro and store promotions are bought through Google Play. We receive a purchase token to confirm the purchase; we never see your payment details.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its ad partners, including Meta Audience Network and Google AdMob. These services may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device model and app usage, to show ads and measure them. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Google Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report: the app version, phone model, Android version and what the app was doing when it stopped. It contains no photos and is used only to find and fix problems. Firebase privacy information.

What we do not do

  • We do not upload or keep your face photos.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your account data, except with the service providers above who run the app for us.

Deleting your data

Email saadfellak0000@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will delete your account data, reviews, tickets and store page within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
