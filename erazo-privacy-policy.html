Privacy Policy — Erazo: AI Object Remover

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what Erazo (“the app”, package com.blayza.photoenhancer) does with your information. The app is published by blayza. For any question, contact saadfellak0000@gmail.com.

The short version

Erazo has no accounts and never uploads your photos. Every edit (removing objects or text, sharpening, restoring faces, colorizing, making a before/after video) runs entirely on your phone with AI models stored inside the app. The only data that leaves your device is what the advertising service below collects.

Your photos

  • The app only sees the photos you pick with Android's photo picker, or share to it.
  • Photos are processed on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Results are saved to your gallery (Pictures/Erazo and Movies/Erazo) only when you tap Save or Video. Shared copies are kept in the app's private cache.
  • To find text in a photo, the app uses Google ML Kit text recognition, which also runs on your device; the photo is not sent to Google.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its ad partners, including Meta Audience Network and Google AdMob. These services may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and app usage, to show ads and measure them. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts or location.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not keep any copy of your photos.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
