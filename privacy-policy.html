Privacy Policy — AR Tracing

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This policy explains what AR Tracing (“the app”) does with your information. The app is published by blayza. If you have any question about this policy, contact saadfellak0000@gmail.com.

The short version

AR Tracing does not have accounts, does not upload your photos, and does not send your camera feed anywhere. Your images stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your device is what the advertising and billing services below collect.

Camera

The app needs camera access to show your paper on screen so a reference image can be placed over it. The camera feed is processed on your device in real time and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. On devices that support ARCore, the app also uses motion and surface data from the camera and sensors to keep the reference image pinned to your paper; this data is used only while the app is open.

Photos you choose

When you import a photo, the app uses the Android photo picker. It receives only the one image you select — it has no access to the rest of your gallery. The selected image is copied into the app's own private storage so it stays in your recent list. These copies never leave your device and are deleted when you remove the item or uninstall the app.

Background removal

The “Cut out” tool uses Google’s ML Kit Subject Segmentation, which runs entirely on your device. Your photo is not uploaded. The first time you use it, Google Play services downloads the model to your phone; after that it works offline.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you are in a region that requires it, the app shows a consent form before any ad is requested, and your choice is respected. Purchasing “Remove ads” or an AR Tracing Pro subscription stops all ad requests.

App settings

The app uses Google Firebase Remote Config to fetch a small set of settings — for example how often an advertisement is shown. To do this, Firebase assigns your installation an anonymous identifier. No personal information is sent, and the identifier is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. The app never sees or stores your payment details. It asks Google Play which products you own so it knows whether to unlock Pro content and hide ads.

Data we do not collect

  • No name, email address, or account of any kind.
  • No precise location.
  • No contacts, messages, files, or other apps' data.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is bundled in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the advertising services above, contact us and we will help you request its removal.

Your choices

  • Revoke camera access at any time in Android Settings → Apps → AR Tracing → Permissions. The app will stop working until it is granted again.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Remove every imported image by long-pressing an item in Recent, or by uninstalling the app.
